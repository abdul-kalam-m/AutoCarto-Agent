**Figure 1.** *Identical data, identical geometry, opposite outcomes.* Tree-canopy loss across 530 Fulton and DeKalb County census tracts (real TIGER geometry, seeded spatially autoregressive variable). **Left:** an unconstrained proposal using equal-interval breaks and a rainbow ramp collapses 414 of 530 tracts (78%) into a single class — the map renders without error and conceals the spatial gradient entirely. **Right:** Gate 2 diagnoses heavy right skew, vetoes the proposal, and prescribes a log transform followed by Jenks natural breaks, yielding balanced classes [98/134/145/90/63] on a colour-vision-safe sequential ramp. The histogram strip shows the mechanism: equal-interval spacing spends classes on a sparse tail, while the prescribed breaks fall where observations actually lie. Note that the rejected classification attains a *higher* goodness-of-variance fit (0.866 vs 0.835); class balance, not GVF, is the honest failure metric here (Section 6.3).

**Figure 2.** *Three-tier architecture and the authority boundary.* Tier 1 (Semantic Engine) reasons over cartographic concepts only; Tier 2 (Deterministic Execution Engine) computes every numeric decision, runs the six validation gates, and renders inside an isolated container; Tier 3 (Data Fabric) performs spatially-first retrieval with metadata quality scoring. The dashed boundary marks the invariant: raw data values never cross into Tier 1, and no numeric constant reaches the executed render without deterministic provenance.

**Figure 3.** *Gate 2 distribution diagnostics across five regimes.* For each regime the gate profiles distribution shape and compares the proposed classification against the prescribed one. The overlay of proposed versus prescribed break positions is the prescription mechanism made visible: rejection returns exact constants, not a critique.

**Figure 4.** *Gate 3b decision triptych.* Strongly coupled, weakly coupled, and independent variable pairs on a queen-contiguity lattice, yielding APPROVE, WARN, and REJECT respectively. On REJECT the gate mandates side-by-side univariate maps — the system declines the map *type*, not merely its styling.
